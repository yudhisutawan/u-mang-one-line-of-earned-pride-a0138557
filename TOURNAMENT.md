# Tournament — One Line of Earned Pride

Judge: the brief published in full in the kit's rules (criteria in order):
1. **Genuine earned pride** — a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene, a small person doing something hard or a long effort finally landing; a line off that subject loses to any entry on the subject.
2. **Concrete scene with a real person** — specific enough that the moment is seen happening rather than summarised.
3. **Pride earned inside the line** — by effort or difficulty the reader can feel, never asserted, congratulated or explained.
4. **Lands in one read** — the pride arrives by the last word, with no setup the reader has to assemble.
5. **Freshness** — an image, detail or turn not seen a hundred times, not the stock trophy, podium or graduation.

Method: every pair judged on the criteria in order — the first criterion where they differ decides, unless a later one differs by much more. Margin 1 = by a hair, 5 = overwhelmingly. 10 candidates = 45 games. No candidate uses the word "proud" or any stock trophy / podium / graduation image. Hermes was unavailable for this bounty (SSH to the VPS broke), so all 10 candidates were built locally to compensate.

## Winner: C — Leo (9–0, undefeated)

> Leo, who has stuttered through every roll call since first grade, reads the last page aloud without stopping once, and the whole class forgets to breathe until he does.

Why it won, against the brief:
1. **Genuine earned pride** — Leo himself does the hard thing; the pride is his own, not watched second-hand (this is what beat G and I, the two strongest challengers).
2. **Concrete scene, real person** — a named boy, a classroom, roll call, the last page: the moment is seen happening, not summarised.
3. **Earned inside the line** — the stutter is a difficulty felt in the body, carried "since first grade", and overcome live in the scene ("without stopping once"). Nothing is asserted or congratulated; no "proud" is ever said.
4. **Lands in one read** — the pride arrives at the last words: the whole class holding its breath until he breathes. No setup left to assemble.
5. **Freshness** — no trophy, podium or graduation; "the whole class forgets to breathe until he does" is its own turn.

Runner-up I (dishwasher, 8–1) lost only to C, on criterion 3: a lifelong bodily obstacle overcome live beats professional practice. No rebuild was needed: the winner beat all nine rivals head-to-head, including the strongest challenger.
